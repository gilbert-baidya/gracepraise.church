Overview Our story Mission Beliefs Values Leadership
Our mission

To glorify God by making disciples.

We love Jesus, love each other, and love our community through worship, teaching, service, and a life of faith.

Our vision

A church where every person experiences the transforming love of Jesus Christ.

We celebrate cultural diversity and live out faith through authentic worship, biblical teaching, and compassionate service.

How we live it

Purpose becomes practice.

These commitments are already part of GPBC's stated mission and describe the shape of life together.

Worship

Worship God with all our hearts in both English and Bangla.

Families

Build strong, Christ-centered families.

Equipping

Equip believers for ministry and service.

Community

Reach our community with the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

Heritage

Celebrate Bangladeshi heritage while embracing all cultures.

Service

Serve the needs of our local and global community.

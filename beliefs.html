What we believe

Anchored in the truth of Christ.

The fundamental truths of the Christian faith that guide us in worship, community, and life.

About GPBC
Overview Our story Mission Beliefs Position papers Values
A clear foundation

We believe in the fundamental truths of the Christian faith. Read each conviction in its own space, without a wall of text.

The Bible

We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible Word of God and our ultimate authority for faith and life.

The Trinity

We believe in one God eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus Christ is the Son of God, born of a virgin, lived a sinless life, died for our sins, rose from the dead, and will return again.

Salvation

We believe salvation is by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone.

The Church

We believe in the universal Church, the body of Christ, made up of all true believers.

Baptism & Communion

We practice believer's baptism and regular communion as ordinances established by Christ.

Eternal Life

We believe in the resurrection of both the saved and the lost—the saved to eternal life and the lost to eternal separation from God.

New Here? You Are Welcome [নতুন এসেছেন? স্বাগতম]

We're so glad you're here! Whether this is your first time or you're just curious about what we're about, you belong. No pressure, no expectations—just a warm welcome waiting for you. [আপনাকে স্বাগতম বার্তা]

What is the service like? [সেবা কেমন?]
Think of it as a time to sing, pray, and learn together. You'll hear uplifting music, a down-to-earth message from the Bible, and feel the care of people who are genuinely happy you're there. [সেবার বিবরণ]
What language is used? [কোন ভাষা ব্যবহার করা হয়?]
We use both Bengali and English throughout our services, making everyone feel at home whether they're more comfortable in one language or the other. [বাংলা এবং ইংরেজি উভয়]
How long is the service? [সেবা কতক্ষণ?]
Services typically last about 90 minutes. We know life is busy, so we make every moment count while respecting your time. [প্রায় ৯০ মিনিট]
What should I expect when I arrive?
Just come as you are—no dress code, no need to know anything in advance. You'll see friendly faces ready to help you find a seat. Don't worry about doing anything "wrong"—there's no wrong way to visit us!

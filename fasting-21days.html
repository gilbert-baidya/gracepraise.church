⛰️ 21 Days Fasting

A Journey of Breakthrough and Spiritual Renewal

"In those days I, Daniel, was mourning three full weeks. I ate no pleasant food, no meat or wine came into my mouth, nor did I anoint myself at all, till three whole weeks were fulfilled."

— Daniel 10:2-3 (NKJV)

🚧 Coming Soon

We are preparing a powerful 21-day fasting devotional based on Daniel's three-week consecration period. This spiritual discipline brings breakthrough in prayer and divine revelation.

The Daniel Fast is a partial fast focusing on spiritual seeking while maintaining nutrition. Join us for this transformative journey of prayer, worship, and consecration.

What This Fasting Journey Will Include:

  • 📖 Daily Scripture readings focused on breakthrough
  • 🙏 Guided prayers for specific areas of need
  • 💭 Reflection questions for spiritual growth
  • 🍎 Daniel Fast dietary guidelines and recipes
  • ✨ Testimonies of breakthrough through fasting
  • 🎯 Weekly focus themes: Surrender, Purification, Breakthrough

📌 Biblical Foundation:

Daniel's 21-day fast was a time of deep spiritual seeking that resulted in angelic visitation and prophetic revelation. This fast is ideal for those seeking God's direction, breakthrough in spiritual warfare, or deeper intimacy with Christ.

GPBC FASTING GUIDE

21-Day Prayer & Fasting Guide

Daniel’s Fast: seeking God with focus and faith.

A sacred guide for spiritual warfare, divine direction, and deeper intimacy with Christ.

Guiding Scripture

Three weeks of seeking God

“In those days I, Daniel, was mourning three full weeks. I ate no pleasant food, no meat or wine came into my mouth, nor did I anoint myself at all, till three whole weeks were fulfilled.”

— Daniel 10:2–3 (NKJV)
Guide in preparation

The 21-day devotional guide is being prepared

This page preserves the prayerful direction and fasting guidance for Daniel’s Fast. A complete day-by-day reader is not available yet.

Use this as a focused guide for preparation and prayer; do not treat it as a published daily journey.

The purpose

The power of Daniel’s Fast

Daniel’s 21-day fast was a time of deep spiritual seeking that resulted in angelic visitation and prophetic revelation. This guide is for those seeking God’s direction, breakthrough in spiritual warfare, or deeper intimacy with Christ.

01

Spiritual Warfare

Break through spiritual opposition and seek victory in areas where you have been battling.

02

Divine Revelation

Seek clarity, wisdom, and prophetic insight for the decisions and challenges you face.

03

Deeper Intimacy

Draw closer to God through consecration and experience His presence.

Guide scope

What this guide includes

  • Daily Scripture readings focused on breakthrough and spiritual victory.
  • Guided prayers for specific areas of spiritual warfare.
  • Reflection questions for personal spiritual growth.
  • Daniel Fast guidelines with dietary recommendations and recipes.
  • Testimonies of breakthrough through fasting.
  • Weekly themes: Surrender, Purification, and Breakthrough.

Prayerful progression

Three weeks of transformation

Week 1

Surrender

Lay down your burdens, desires, and plans at the feet of Jesus. Learn to trust God completely as you begin this journey of consecration.

Week 2

Purification

Allow God’s refining fire to purify your heart, mind, and spirit. Seek His face in spiritual cleansing and renewal.

Week 3

Breakthrough

Stand in faith as God brings breakthrough in your circumstances. Receive revelation, healing, and answers to prayer.

Important note

Fast with wisdom

The Daniel Fast is a partial fast focusing on vegetables, fruits, whole grains, nuts, and water while abstaining from meat, dairy, sweets, and processed foods. Always consult with your doctor before beginning any fast, especially if you have health conditions. This fast is about spiritual seeking, not physical deprivation.

New Here? You Are Welcome [নতুন এসেছেন? স্বাগতম]

We're so glad you're here! Whether this is your first time or you're just curious about what we're about, you belong. No pressure, no expectations—just a warm welcome waiting for you. [আপনাকে স্বাগতম বার্তা]

What is the service like? [সেবা কেমন?]
Think of it as a time to sing, pray, and learn together. You'll hear uplifting music, a down-to-earth message from the Bible, and feel the care of people who are genuinely happy you're there. [সেবার বিবরণ]
What language is used? [কোন ভাষা ব্যবহার করা হয়?]
We use both Bengali and English throughout our services, making everyone feel at home whether they're more comfortable in one language or the other. [বাংলা এবং ইংরেজি উভয়]
How long is the service? [সেবা কতক্ষণ?]
Services typically last about 90 minutes. We know life is busy, so we make every moment count while respecting your time. [প্রায় ৯০ মিনিট]
What should I expect when I arrive?
Just come as you are—no dress code, no need to know anything in advance. You'll see friendly faces ready to help you find a seat. Don't worry about doing anything "wrong"—there's no wrong way to visit us!

Whether you're new to church or returning after time away, you'll find a warm welcome here.

Our Father in Heaven

When Jesus taught us to pray, He revealed seven aspects of our Heavenly Father's nature. Each dimension reflects His character, His kingdom, and His perfect will for our lives. This sacred wheel invites us to meditate on who our Father truly is.

Character
Kingdom
Will
Provision
Forgiveness
Guidance
Protection

The Father's Character

Matthew 6:9

"Our Father in heaven, hallowed be Your name" — He is holy, loving, and worthy of reverence.

The Father's Kingdom

Matthew 6:10a

"Your kingdom come" — He reigns with justice, peace, and eternal authority.

The Father's Will

Matthew 6:10b

"Your will be done" — His plans are perfect, trustworthy, and for our good.

The Father's Provision

Matthew 6:11

"Give us today our daily bread" — He sustains us with everything we need.

The Father's Forgiveness

Matthew 6:12

"Forgive us our debts" — His mercy is boundless, cleansing us completely.

The Father's Guidance

Matthew 6:13a

"Lead us not into temptation" — He guides our steps and guards our hearts.

The Father's Protection

Matthew 6:13b

"Deliver us from evil" — He is our shield, our refuge, and our strong tower.

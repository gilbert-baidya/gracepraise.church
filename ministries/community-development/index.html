Community Development

Building stronger families and a healthier community through Christ's love.

Community Development Ministry strengthens families and supports practical needs in our local area through encouragement and resource connection.

We believe the church should bless the city—not only with words, but also with action.

As GPBC grows, we aim to develop programs that bring stability, wisdom, and hope to families and youth.

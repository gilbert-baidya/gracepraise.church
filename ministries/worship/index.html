Worship Ministry

Glorifying God together—through praise, prayer, and reverence.

Worship Ministry helps the church lift up the name of Jesus with pure hearts. Worship is not performance—it is our response to God's goodness and salvation.

Through singing, instruments, and Scripture-centered worship, we create space for the congregation to unite as one body and honor Christ.

If God has given you gifts in singing, music, media, or sound, we welcome you to serve with excellence and a servant heart.

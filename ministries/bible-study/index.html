Bible Study

Learning God's Word together—clearly, deeply, and practically.

Bible Study is where we slow down and let God's Word shape our mind, heart, and daily life.

We ask questions, learn context, and encourage one another to practice the Word at home, at work, and in relationships.

Our prayer is that GPBC becomes a Word-rooted church where Scripture is not only heard, but lived.

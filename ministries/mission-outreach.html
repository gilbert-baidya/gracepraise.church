Mission Outreach

Making Disciples of All Nations

The Great Commission

Mission Outreach exists to fulfill the Great Commission by proclaiming the Gospel both locally and globally. We believe that every Christian is called to be a witness for Christ, and that the Church exists not for itself, but for the salvation of the world.

Through local evangelism, international mission trips, church planting partnerships, and strategic outreach initiatives, we seek to make disciples of all nations. Our mission efforts include sharing the Gospel through word and deed, training believers for ministry, and planting churches where none exist.

Whether you are called to go, to send, to pray, or to give, this ministry provides opportunities for every believer to participate in the mission of God to redeem a people for His glory from every corner of the earth.

"Therefore go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, and teaching them to obey everything I have commanded you."

— Matthew 28:19-20 (NIV)

The ministry’s purpose

Carry hope beyond our walls

Mission Outreach helps GPBC look beyond its walls with the good news of Jesus Christ and practical love for neighbors near and far. We seek to serve with humility, build relationships, and participate in God’s work across communities.

Specific partnerships and opportunities should be confirmed with the church before making plans or commitments.

“Therefore go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit.”

Matthew 28:19–20 (NIV)

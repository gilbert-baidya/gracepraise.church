Hospital Ministry

Bringing comfort and hope to the sick.

✦

Worship, growth, service, and reach—lived together.

The ministry’s purpose

A ministry of presence

Hospital Ministry offers compassionate presence, prayer, and encouragement to people who are sick or recovering. It reflects Jesus’ invitation to care for people in vulnerable seasons with humility and hope.

Current visiting and volunteer arrangements should be confirmed with GPBC so care can be offered appropriately and respectfully.

“I was sick and you looked after me… whatever you did for one of the least of these brothers and sisters of mine, you did for me.”

Matthew 25:36, 40 (NIV)

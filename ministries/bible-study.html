The ministry’s purpose

Discover the riches of Scripture

The Bible Study Ministry exists to help believers grow in their knowledge of Scripture and deepen their relationship with Christ. Through careful study and faithful teaching of God’s Word, we are equipped to live out faith with wisdom and conviction.

Whether you are new to faith or have walked with Christ for years, this ministry welcomes you to discover Scripture with other believers and be changed by its truth.

“All Scripture is God-breathed and is useful for teaching, rebuking, correcting and training in righteousness.”

2 Timothy 3:16–17 (NIV)

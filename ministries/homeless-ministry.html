The ministry’s purpose

Make room for dignity and hope

Homeless Ministry is a practical expression of Christ’s compassion toward neighbors experiencing housing insecurity. The ministry encourages care that sees people clearly, honors their dignity, and responds with prayer and tangible support.

Because specific opportunities can change, please contact GPBC for current ways to participate or request support.

Hospital Ministry

Bringing comfort, prayer, and hope to the sick and suffering.

Hospital Ministry is a ministry of presence—showing up when life feels heavy. We visit, pray, and encourage people who are sick or recovering.

We believe prayer matters and Scripture brings strength, and that God comforts His children in every season.

If you or a loved one needs prayer or a visit, please let us know. We want no one to suffer alone.

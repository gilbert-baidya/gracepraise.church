Mission Outreach

Reaching the lost—locally and globally—with the love of Jesus.

Mission Outreach is the heartbeat of the Great Commission at GPBC. We share Christ through evangelism, service, and invitation.

Outreach is love in motion. We listen, serve, pray, and speak the gospel with gentleness and courage.

Whether local or global, we want to be a church that reaches people faithfully.

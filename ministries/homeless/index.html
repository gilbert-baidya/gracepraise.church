Homeless Ministry

Serving with dignity—food, care, prayer, and friendship.

Homeless Ministry serves people with dignity, compassion, and practical support through care packages, food help, and prayer.

We believe every person is created in God's image and deserves respect and love.

If you need assistance or want to serve, we invite you to be part of what God is doing through GPBC.

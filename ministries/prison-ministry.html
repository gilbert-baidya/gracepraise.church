The ministry’s purpose

Remember those who are captive

Prison Ministry carries the hope of Jesus Christ to people who are incarcerated and to families affected by incarceration. It is a ministry of listening, prayer, Scripture, dignity, and patient restoration.

Because access and partner requirements vary, contact GPBC for current opportunities and guidance.

“Continue to remember those in prison as if you were together with them in prison.”

Hebrews 13:3 (NIV)

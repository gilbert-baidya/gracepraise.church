Prison Ministry

Restoration, Hope, and Forgiveness in Christ

Ministry of Restoration

The Prison Ministry exists to bring the hope of the Gospel to those who are incarcerated. We believe that no one is beyond the reach of God's grace, and that Christ offers forgiveness, transformation, and a new beginning to all who turn to Him—regardless of their past.

Through regular visits, Bible studies, prayer, and correspondence, we seek to remind those in prison that they are not forgotten by God or His people. We share the message that true freedom is found not in circumstances, but in Christ, who sets the captives free.

This ministry also supports families affected by incarceration and helps prepare individuals for reentry into society with dignity, faith, and hope for a transformed life.

"Continue to remember those in prison as if you were together with them in prison, and those who are mistreated as if you yourselves were suffering."

— Hebrews 13:3 (NIV)

Bring Hope to the Incarcerated

Join us in sharing the Gospel and offering restoration to those behind bars.

Prison Ministry

Proclaiming hope, restoration, and Christ's forgiveness.

Prison Ministry shares the gospel where hope is often forgotten. We believe no one is beyond the reach of Christ.

This ministry offers prayer, Scripture encouragement, and discipleship support, and also stands with families impacted by incarceration.

We serve with humility and wisdom, trusting God to transform lives from the inside out.

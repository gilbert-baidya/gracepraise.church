Kids Ministry

Nurturing the Next Generation in Faith and Love

Our Vision

Kids Ministry exists to introduce children to Jesus Christ and help them grow in faith through age-appropriate teaching, worship, and fellowship. We believe that every child is precious to God and deserves to hear the gospel in a way they can understand and receive.

Through Sunday School, special events, Bible stories, worship songs, and interactive activities, we create a safe and joyful environment where children can learn about God's love, discover biblical truths, and develop a personal relationship with Jesus.

We partner with parents to disciple the next generation, equipping them with godly character, biblical wisdom, and a heart for worship and service.

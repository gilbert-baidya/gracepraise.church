Worship, growth, service, and reach—lived together.

The ministry’s purpose

Nurture the next generation

Kids Ministry exists to introduce children to Jesus Christ and help them grow in faith through age-appropriate teaching, worship, and fellowship. Every child is precious to God and deserves to hear the Gospel in a way they can understand and receive.

The ministry description names Bible stories, worship songs, interactive activities, and partnership with parents as ways children develop godly character, biblical wisdom, and a heart for worship and service.

“Let the little children come to me, and do not hinder them, for the kingdom of heaven belongs to such as these.”

Matthew 19:14 (NIV)

Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my gift tax-deductible?

Yes! Grace and Praise Bangladeshi Church is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. All donations are tax-deductible to the full extent allowed by law. You will receive a receipt for your records at the end of each year or upon request.

Can I give monthly or set up recurring donations?

Yes! You can set up recurring monthly donations through PayPal, or contact our office to arrange automatic bank transfers. Monthly giving helps us plan and sustain ministry throughout the year and ensures consistent support for our programs.

Who can I contact if I need help or have questions?

If you have questions about giving or need assistance, please contact us at info@gracepraise.church or bangladeshi.church@gmail.com. We’re here to help!

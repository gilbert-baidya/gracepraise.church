Who we are

A spiritual home for the Bangladeshi community—and a welcoming church family for all.

We are a vibrant Christian community where people worship, grow, and serve together. Our Bangladeshi roots are part of our story; our welcome is wider than our heritage.

Grace and Praise Bangladeshi Church was founded with a vision to create a spiritual home for the Bangladeshi community in California. We continue to welcome families from all backgrounds who seek to grow in their faith and serve God together.

Where we came from

From a small gathering to a thriving spiritual family.

Our journey began with a small group of believers who gathered to worship God and celebrate their cultural heritage. Over time, GPBC has grown into a community that embraces both Bangladeshi roots and life as followers of Christ.

Our commitments

  • ✦ Worship God with all our hearts in English and Bangla.
  • ✦ Build strong, Christ-centered families.
  • ✦ Equip believers for ministry and service.
  • ✦ Reach our community with the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

Our vision

A church where every person experiences the transforming love of Jesus Christ.

We envision a church where cultural diversity is celebrated and faith is lived out through authentic worship, biblical teaching, and compassionate service.

What we believe

The truths that guide our faith and life together.

Our beliefs are not a checklist; they are the foundation beneath our worship, our teaching, and our hope.

Read all our beliefs →
01 The Bible +

We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible Word of God and our ultimate authority for faith and life.

02 The Trinity +

We believe in one God eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

03 Jesus Christ +

We believe Jesus Christ is the Son of God, born of a virgin, lived a sinless life, died for our sins, rose from the dead, and will return again.

04 Salvation +

We believe salvation is by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone.

Our Beginning

Grace and Praise Bangladeshi Church was founded with a vision to create a spiritual home for the Bangladeshi community in California. Our journey began with a small group of believers who gathered to worship God and celebrate their cultural heritage.

Over the years, we have grown into a vibrant community that embraces both our Bangladeshi roots and our identity as followers of Christ. We continue to welcome families from all backgrounds who seek to grow in their faith and serve God together.

We believe in being clear about where we stand on important theological and practical issues. Our position papers provide detailed explanations of our beliefs and practices on various topics.

Key Positions Include:

  • 📜 Statement of Faith
    Our comprehensive declaration of biblical beliefs and doctrines
  • 💑 Marriage and Family
    Biblical perspectives on marriage, family, and relationships
  • 🕊️ The Sanctity of Life
    Our commitment to honoring and protecting all human life
  • 🌏 Cultural Integration and Heritage
    Celebrating Bangladeshi culture while embracing all people
  • 🤲 Community Engagement
    How we serve and impact our local community

For more detailed information about our position papers, please contact our church office.

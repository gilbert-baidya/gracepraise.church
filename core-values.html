Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Our Beginning

Grace and Praise Bangladeshi Church was founded with a vision to create a spiritual home for the Bangladeshi community in California. Our journey began with a small group of believers who gathered to worship God and celebrate their cultural heritage.

Over the years, we have grown into a vibrant community that embraces both our Bangladeshi roots and our identity as followers of Christ. We continue to welcome families from all backgrounds who seek to grow in their faith and serve God together.

These values guide everything we do as a church community. They shape our worship, our relationships, and our service to God and others.

🙏 WORSHIP

We worship God with passion and authenticity in both English and Bangla

📖 WORD

We are committed to biblical teaching and learning God's Word

❤️ LOVE

We love God with all our hearts and love people unconditionally

🤝 COMMUNITY

We build authentic relationships and support one another as family

🌍 OUTREACH

We reach out to our community and the world with the Gospel of Jesus

🌟 EXCELLENCE

We honor God by pursuing excellence in all we do
